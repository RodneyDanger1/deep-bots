# Format shapes

Each shape gives its parts, in order, and its limits. Use the shape as written; do not add parts.

## Email

Parts: subject, greeting, ask or news, reason, next step, sign-off.

Limits: subject at most 8 words; body at most 150 words; one ask; at most one link.

- Subject names the matter, not a mood: `Invoice 4021 needs approval by Friday`.
- One greeting line, with the recipient's name when it is known.
- The ask or the news in the first sentence after the greeting.
- The next step names who does what and when.
- No `I hope this finds you well`, no signature block beyond the name, no tracking pixel.

## Decision brief

Parts: decision, why now, options, recommendation, next action.

Limits: 400 words; options table at most 4 rows; recommendation at most 3 sentences.

- The decision is one sentence, with its deadline.
- The options table names cost, risk, and source for each option.
- The recommendation says what to do, not what could be done.
- The next action names an owner and a date.

## Social post

Parts: hook line, two to four short lines, takeaway, optional link.

Limits: 120 words; at most two hashtags; one link.

- The hook line is a fact or a claim, not a teaser question.
- Each line stands alone; no thread numbering inside one post.
- The takeaway is the one sentence a reader should remember.
- No engagement bait, no `like if`, no countdown.

## Deck copy

Parts: title, at most three bullets, source line.

Limits: at most 6 slides; at most 12 words per bullet; speaker notes separate from slide text.

- The title is the claim of the slide, not its topic.
- A bullet carries a fact or an instruction, never a paragraph.
- The source line goes under the bullet it supports.
- Inline assets only: no script tag, no remote font, no remote image, no embed.

## Chat reply

Parts: answer, then the one reason or caveat.

Limits: 3 sentences, 60 words; no greeting; no signature.

- The answer comes first, in one sentence.
- A missing fact is `not found`, with the one action that would settle it.
- No offer of further help as a closing line.

## Handoff note

Parts: status, result, evidence, unblock.

Limits: 150 words; one status word; evidence as a path with a line, or a command with its output.

- Status is one of `done`, `blocked`, `failed`, `not found`.
- Result is the outcome, not the activity.
- Evidence is quotable and specific.
- A blocked note ends with the one action that unblocks it.
