name: writing-formats
description: Shape a message for a fixed format such as email, decision brief, post, deck copy, chat reply, or handoff note. Use when the output shape and its limits matter more than the wording, and the reader needs one clear ask. Never sends, posts, or publishes, never invents a recipient, price, date, or commitment, and never changes a quotation or required disclosure.
---

# When to use

- Use when the user names a format, or the channel makes the shape clear. Examples: a client email, a feed post, deck copy, a handoff note.
- Use together with `writing-brand` for voice. This skill controls the shape and the limits; the voice skill controls the sentences.
- Do not use for a free-form report with no channel, or for a chat answer that is one line.

# Steps

1. **Name the format and the ask.** Read `references/format-shapes.md`, pick the shape, and name the one action the reader should take.
   - Validate: the chosen shape lists its required parts; a missing part is named as absent before drafting.
2. **Set the budget before drafting.** Subject, body, list, and slide limits from the shape.
   - Validate: the budget is written down, and each limit is a number.
3. **Draft the opening line.** The first line carries the news or the ask.
   - Validate: the first line contains an ask or a result; no greeting-only or weather-only opening.
4. **Fill the shape's parts in order.** Each part gets one fact or one request.
   - Validate: every required part is present, and no part holds two asks.
5. **Apply the voice and claim rules.** Run the voice pass from `writing-brand` and keep every claim inside the approved brief.
   - Validate: no banned phrase survives, and each claim traces to an approved fact.
6. **Check the shape reads in five seconds.** One ask, one action, no wall of text.
   - Validate: a reader can answer `what do you want me to do` from the first two lines and the closing line.
7. **Label and list what is missing.** Write `draft - not sent`, and name anything the sender must supply: recipient, price, date, attachment.
   - Validate: the label is present, and the missing list is empty or explicit.

# Refusals

- Never send, post, schedule, or publish. Produce text only.
- Never invent a recipient, a price, a date, a deadline, or a commitment on the sender's behalf.
- Never change a quotation, a legal line, or a required disclosure to fit the format.
- Never put a script, a remote asset, or a tracking pixel in deck copy; inline assets only.
- Never write a subject line that misstates the body to raise opens.
- Never produce a format that hides the ask at the end of a long page.

# Checks

1. Every required part of the chosen shape is present and in order.
2. Each budget limit is met, measured rather than estimated.
3. Every claim traces to an approved fact, and no banned phrase survives.
4. One ask, one action, and a reader can find both in five seconds.
5. Unsent output carries `draft - not sent`, and the sender's missing items are listed.
